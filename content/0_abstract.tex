\section*{Abstract}
\label{sec:abstract}

With the increasing accessibility of consumer-grade 3D printers, an ever-growing number of users are engaging in designing and fabricating their own models. 
Despite this trend, the transition from digital model to printed object still involves several steps that require technical knowledge and repetitive manual configuration. 
In particular, defining and reapplying material and printing parameters across multiple design iterations often results in redundant effort and increased potential for error.

To address this issue, the presented concept introduces the idea of `print hints' embedded directly within the 3D model to retain information about relevant printing parameters. 
These hints enable a more seamless workflow by linking model components to specific presets and allowing for automatic or semi-automatic reassignment of parameters during the import into slicer software. 
This approach reduces cognitive load, minimises repetitive tasks, and supports a more efficient and user-friendly iterative design process, while preserving user control and flexibility.
